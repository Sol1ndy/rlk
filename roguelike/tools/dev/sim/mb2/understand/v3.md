### 7.4 Wing poses and motion
* **Idle hover**: slow flap between **V-up** (forewings ≈ 20–30° off vertical, tips ≈ 1.1 H_b above the crest) and **open** (≈ 60°) every
  ≈ 0.65–1.0 s (13–20 t). Ribbons sway with a lag; the body bobs ≈ ±0.05 H_b.
* **Big flap** (날갯짓 tell): V-up ↔ **flat horizontal** (90°, span ≈ 3 H_b). Down-stroke ≈ 0.25 s (5 t), up-stroke ≈ 0.2 s (4 t).
* **Swing** (날개탄): the whole body spins 270–360° about the vertical axis, with one forewing held flat and leading like a blade.
* **Glide**: wings flat, body pitched 30–60°. **Dive**: body near horizontal and head-first, wings swept back and up.
* **Fold up** (강화 해제 start): both forewings pressed together vertically above the back (closed butterfly), ribbons trailing.
* **Forward wrap / ball** (코코볼 charge and ball): forewings curl forward around the front into a ring (charge) or a closed ball ≈ 1.2–1.4 H_b
  across. Glass faces outward and the frame hooks curl underneath.
* **Cocoon wrap** (release): forewings wrap around the body like a cage, capped by the shoulder plates on top. This is the same idea as the
  entrance cocoon in v1, at ≈ 1.3–1.4 H_b.
* **Dissolve** (38.10+): the wings break from the edges inward into lime flame-like shards and the ribbons shrink. This is the transition
  back to human form (finish in v4).
* **Element switch**: swap the glass, ribbon and lobe textures in 1 tick, add the rim glow and mauve-tint the frame. The geometry is identical.
