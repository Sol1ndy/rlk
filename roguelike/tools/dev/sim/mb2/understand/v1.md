  centre under the cocoon (#81C4AB centre, #BCDDD2 rim, `k_f0000`).

  verdant froglight under moss for the glow ring, prismarine/warped-wart-blue walls, a spiral of light-blue/prismarine for the
  ceiling, sea lanterns/pearlescent froglight for blue light.
