  `hp_player_0-40.png`, `hp_player_wingstrike_35.0-36.4.png`.
* Scale: H ≈ 2.6 m (helmet top → feet); antenna tips ≈ 3.4 m; human-form stance width ≈ 0.35 H; butterfly wingspan
  tip-to-tip ≈ 3 H ≈ 7.5–8 m in-game (2.3 H in the PDF render, whose wings are swept back). Details in §5.

  cave mouth in the distance. Floor is flat; no telegraph decals are ever drawn on it in v2.
