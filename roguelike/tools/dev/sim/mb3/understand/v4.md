## 1. Boss identity, nameplate, scale

* Nameplate: **브레게 프로토타입** / **실험성 역장 발생 장치**, **Lv.93**, red fill #FC5A57 (sampled #FB5656 … #FF575F), bar
  x 712–1209 px. Segment T: fill to x 1097 → **77 %**; segment G: 100 % from start to end (no damage dealt in this take).
* Party: 느비예트, 리니, 백출, 산고노미야 코코미 (active, Lv.90, 14642/33832). Quest marker 훈련장으로 가기 5270–5275 m.
* **Scale — read carefully, v1 and v2 disagree and v4 sides with v1:**
  * v2 measured the ring outer diameter **D ≈ 5.0 m** from the player standing next to the grounded device (`v2/full/f1526.png`:
    ring 535 px vs player 190 px; equal depth assumed).
  * v1 measured **D ≈ 7.5–8 m** with a horizon-fit camera model.
  * v4: the blast disc radius is robust in device units — **R ≈ 1.7 D (1.6–1.9)**: rim half-width 815 px (f255, x 195–1825)
    vs the ring's 390–400 px at the same depth, corrected by the perspective factor √(1−k²) with k = R/Z ≈ 0.46–0.62.
  * v4 also gives a metric bound independent of the focal length: Kokomi standing (1.6–1.7 m) is 145–165 px tall just
    **outside** the rim's near edge, while the rim's widest point (farther than her) is 810–815 px from the axis → **R ≥ 8.3–9.3 m**.
    With any plausible Genshin camera (focal 1000–1600 px, vertical FOV 40–55°) the widest point is 0.5 R farther than the
    player and **R ≈ 11–14 m**, i.e. **D ≈ 6.5–8 m** (agrees with v1). v2's D = 5 m is most likely low because the player stood
    1–3 m in front of the grounded device.
  * Recommendation for the build: keep every size in D units (this file gives them), and choose one D for the whole boss.
* Other proportions in v4 (front view `zoom_f0600.jpg`, ring ≈ 430–460 px): halo top → pendulum tip ≈ 0.9–1.0 D; at hover the
  ring centre is ≈ 0.55–0.6 D above the floor and the pendulum tip ≈ 0.1 D above it (hittable from the ground).
* Locomotion in v4: **none** — the device hovers over the central gold emblem for the whole of segment G; it only yaws, rolls
  (≈ 10–25°), and moves its parts.
