* Large **round open-air plaza** of cream marble (#DAD2D1 / #E2DCDB / #CEC6C4, moss streaks #BDBDAC, green moss tufts) inside a
  ring of ruined grey-lilac stone walls with arches, pillars and blue-glass windows (#575A75 / #696B84 shade, #94939E light),
  ivy, mountains and pines beyond. Floor inlays: **two concentric octagonal rings** (inlaid lighter stone bands) and a **gold
  inlaid central emblem** (cross/sun with radiating gold lines and concentric circles; #BCA189 / #90776A, gold ≈ #C9A35A). The
  boss sits over the emblem at rest and **returns to it for the field state**. Floor radius clearly ≥ 20 m.
* MC suggestion: circle r ≈ 20 blocks of smooth quartz / calcite / pale birch-free stone with diorite/polished andesite bands for
  the octagons, a gold emblem of yellow/orange terracotta + gold blocks + light-weighted-pressure-plate inlay at the centre,
  stone-brick/tuff-brick ruined walls with blue stained glass.
