  ruined colonnades (lilac-grey stone #A28373, cream #D6CCC0 / #ECD4CE floor, blue-teal at night), trees and mountains beyond.

  warm stone #C9A27E est.). The shockwave rings spread over the lower floor only; the gallery is a refuge (caption "2층
  활용").
* MC suggestion: lower floor r ≈ 18 blocks of smooth sandstone / calcite with polished andesite + gold-block inlay rings and
  a gold compass at the centre; an outer raised ring walkway 4 blocks up (r 18–21) with stone-brick balustrades and pillars,
  reachable by stairs (refuge for 인력 충격파).

## 5. Field break / groggy and the 20-hit replacement (user requirement)

* **Our game has no Pneuma/Ousia: replace it with a hit meter of ≈ 20 hits** (user: "타수를 채워야 해결되도록 해. 한 20타정도?").
  Suggested implementation:
  * Meter appears when the field starts (bossbar segment or actionbar "역장 ▮▮▮▯▯ 12/20"); every player melee/projectile hit
    on the boss (or only the core hitbox under the ring, ≈ sphere r 1.2 m at ≈ 2–3 m height — reachable by low-gravity jumps)
