## 3. Mechanics notes for the build

* **감소 영역 (바닥 뜯기)**: the PDF calls it easy on its own but dangerous chained with others; v3 shows it chained straight
  into 파쇄 (end of eruptions → 2.5 s → 파쇄 dip). Suggested MC (1:1 metres = blocks):
  * Activation 22 t: core drum turns to face the target, medallions slide out to ±4 b and gold beam lines (`dust` +
    `end_rod` line) appear between them and the core; `block.beacon.activate` cue. The boss is not stationary-locked but barely
    moves.
  * Then **9 events over ≈ 210 t**, interval rand(11–50 t), mean 26 t. Each event: 1–3 telegraph discs **r 2.8** (dark
    stone-brown fill + thin pale-gold rim, cracks brighten) placed at the player's position + random offset 2–7 b (keep at least
    one disc ≥ 1.5 b off the player's exact spot, like the video); **lead 15 t**; eruption = cylinder hurt r 2.8, height 0–3.5,
    one hit, small launch (vy ≈ 0.6). Debris `block_display` chunks rise 3.5–4 b and fall slowly for 20–30 t.
  * Beams end at ≈ Z+206 t, last eruptions can still follow for 40 t.
* **파쇄 (바위 날리기)**: dip toward player 12 t → vent flare 8 t → tower 18 t → flame band 17 t (that is the ≈ 3 s tell) →
  **8 floor bursts, 6 t apart**, each r ≈ 1.5–2 (spike fan 2–3 b tall), first ≈ 5–7 b from the player on the boss's side, each
  next one stepping ≈ 2–3 b along an arc that converges on the player's current position (the last 1–2 land on the player).
  **A burst hit ≈ 25 % of max HP + knockdown ≈ 30 t.** Each burst spawns a slab that rises to 4–7 b over 15 t and hovers;
  from ≈ 40 t after the first burst, throw the slabs one by one every 8 t at the player's position (straight line, speed
  ≈ 2 b/t, hurt box ≈ 3 × 1.2 × 0.8 b, impact r 1.5). Optional take-C variant: the boss glides low (ring 2.5–3 b) beside the
  player during the throws, then rises over 12 t.
  * Caption advice "점프질 해야 피하기 쉽다": bursts come from the floor, so jumping (especially with the low-gravity jump buff in
    the field state) clears them; in MC give the burst hurt volume only 0–1.2 b height for the first 3 t of each burst so a
    well-timed jump avoids it.
* **No hit counter / groggy / Pneuma info in v3.** The user rule (≈ 20 player hits on the core resolve the field state instead
  of 3 Pneuma hits) still applies from v1; v3 neither confirms nor contradicts it.
