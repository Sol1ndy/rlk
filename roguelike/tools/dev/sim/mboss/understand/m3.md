# m3.mp4: frame-accurate analysis (Corrupted Guardian / 파수꾼・타락)

Source: `src/m3.mp4`, 1920x1080, 60 fps, 2372 frames (39.53 s), AAC 48 kHz stereo.
Notation: `s` = seconds in m3, `f` = 60 fps frame index (0-based), `t` = Minecraft tick (20 tps, `t = s*20`).
180 of 2371 frames are exact duplicates (capture repeats), so any timing here is ±2 f (±0.7 t).
All images are in `understand/m3/`. Start with **`m3_keyframes.jpg`** (27 labelled stills) and **`hp_and_timer_m3.png`** (boss HP bar and chaos-timer ring over the whole clip).

**What m3 covers.** m3 is the **end of the fight mechanic**: the end of chaos (#7, 혼돈), destroying the element stars, the **groggy** state, the **get-up**, and a second chaos start with **inert stars** (타성 원소별).
Patterns #1-#6 (thrusts, javelin, slam, pull, fly-slam, guard mode) do **not** appear in m3. Neither do the fail-state spear slams, the entrance or the death.

The HP bar subtitle reads `개안자 / 세계 망루의 파수꾼, Lv.103`. In the PDF, "세계 망루의 파수꾼" is **Melanta (멜란타)**, the named twin of Nihilita (니힐리타). Both are 파수꾼・타락 and the mechanics are the same.

  - Radius is still only roughly bounded at ≈15-25 m (5-8 BH). No shot in m3 shows the full rim.
- **Centre**: every chaos start ends with the boss **hovering over the centre star**, with the element stars on the floor directly beneath her.
- **Camera**: Genshin's follow cam, ≈4-6 m behind and slightly above the player, pitched ≈10-15° down. In G the player stands ≈5-6 m south of the centre and the camera is almost static.
  - Other shots: full-screen burst cinematics (Citlali, Mavuika) in E and H; a camera orbiting the grounded boss during groggy (F); a very low close camera on Mavuika's bike in H.

## 5. Visual details per mechanic

### 5.1 Star destruction → boss damage
See `s_star1break_crop_380-452.jpg`, `s_star2break_crop_40-180.jpg`, `c_960-1080.jpg`, `key_f0404/0416/0984/0993/2098/2104*`.

Sequence, identical every time:
1. The star dies on the floor.
2. **+2 f (1 t)**: a **4-point starburst on the boss's chest**, ≈0.5-0.6 BH (H: ≈2 BH of rays).
3. **+15 f (5 t)**: HP drops and the damage number pops up, together with a **thin ring around her body** (radius ≈0.35-0.5 BH).
4. Elemental sparks for ≈0.5 s.

Star-1 and star-2 amounts are **6 % and 18 % max HP**, no matter which star breaks first. Two stars can die 3 t apart (E), and each still applies its own amount.

Burst and number colours follow the element:

|---|---|---|---|
| Electro (C, B) | magenta-white `#c85dcc` / `#852e83` / `#dbc9ed` / white | violet ring, violet lightning arcs | purple `#b070e0`-ish |

| Inert (H, blue star) | pure white `#fbffff` with violet-grey edges | white ring, white stars and snowflakes | cyan |

### 5.2 Chaos end and fall (2nd star)
- She hangs ≈0.3 s, then **falls ≈1 BH to the floor in ≈0.4-0.5 s**, drifting ≤1 BH sideways. She lands **0.8-1.0 s (16-20 t) after the break**.
- On landing there is no impact AoE and no player damage.
- The crimson **sky dome** fades 0.7-0.9 s after the break (B). In attempt #2 a **crimson floor tint and dark lighting lingered ≈6.8 s** before lifting to day (F).
- The red sigil and the cube streams vanish with the break. No cube streams are visible during groggy.

### 5.7 Chaos ambience
See `r_chaos_crop_452-596.jpg`, `r_cubes_2180-2276.jpg`, `e_1990-2371.jpg`, `key_f1830/2200*`.

- **Floor** (dusk attempts): crimson spreading from the centre, with glowing **orange crack lines** (lava-like veins) and red ring arcs.
- **Cube streams**:

- **Boss in the hover**: static, slow bob. Wings spread wide; the spear lies horizontally behind her shoulders (crescent head to screen right). The halo is hidden behind the sigil.
