# m2.mp4: frame-accurate analysis (Corrupted Guardian / 파수꾼・타락, "Nihilita")

Source: `src/m2.mp4`, 1920x1080, 60 fps, 2402 frames (40.03 s), AAC 48 kHz stereo.
Notation: `s` = seconds in m2, `f` = 60 fps frame index (0-based), `t` = Minecraft tick (20 tps, `t = s*20`).
442 of 2401 frames are exact duplicates (the capture drops or repeats frames), so treat any timing here as ±2 f (±0.7 t).
All images are in `understand/m2/`. Start with **`m2_keyframes.jpg`**: 24 labelled key events.

PDF patterns seen in m2: **#6 파수 모드 (guard mode) three times** (once in clip A, twice in clip B) and **#7 혼돈 (chaos)**, which starts in m2 and runs past its end.
Patterns #1-#5 (thrusts, javelin, slam, pull, fly-slam) do **not** appear in m2. Groggy and death are also not in m2: only the 1st of 2 stars is destroyed, at 39.5 s.

## 1. Scale and measurements

Scale comes from `key_f1085.jpg` (boss and Clorinde side by side; the player is slightly nearer the camera) and from `key_f1740.jpg`.
- **Boss height**: feet to crown ≈ 440 px against the player's 270 px. After a ~1.1× perspective correction this gives **≈1.8 player heights ≈ 3.0 m**. With the gold halo/crown spikes she reaches ≈ **3.4 m** (≈2.05 player heights). In the rest of this file, **1 BH (boss height) ≈ 3.0 m**.
- **Spear**: ≈1.4-1.5 BH ≈ **4.3-4.5 m**, measured on the horizontal spear in `key_f600` and `key_f1000`: tip x≈100-270 to hand x≈700-830, plus the butt. The head is gold, with a red/black four-point-star core and swept-back gold "wing" blades (the crescent), about 25 % of the total length. The shaft is gold.
- **Wings**: white feathers on her left (screen right in front views), with a large gold winged pauldron/shield whose gem stars glow. Folded wing length ≈0.7 BH. In the guard pose they spread up and out to ≈1.0-1.3 BH.
- **Her right arm** (screen left): crimson/black, jagged, "corrupted" (dark red `#5a3232` with red-black voxels). Her lower body is a long white-feather skirt with red/black legs and a red heart-shaped foot plate.
- She **floats or glides**. She never shows a walk cycle: she slides along the ground with a slight hover.
- **Boss HP bar**: top centre. Title `개안자`, subtitle `세계 망루의 파수꾼`, `Lv.103`. Red fill `#fa585b`, spanning x≈725-1199 (474 px) at y≈81-93.
- **HP over m2**: clip A ≈97 %; clip B starts at 100 %, 98.9 % at 20 s, 97.9 % at 30 s.
- **Star kill**: when the 1st star is destroyed, the bar drops 29 px (1189→1160) = **-6.1 % of max HP** at 39.683 s (f2381, t793.7). This matches the PDF's 6 %. The damage number is purple `43873`.

## 2. Arena and camera

- **Arena**: a large **circular stone plaza** of pale lilac-white marble. Day colours `#d2d2d2` / `#e6e6e6` / `#dcdcda`; night `#5e788f` / `#6e8296`.
  - The floor has thin concentric grooves (`#dedddb`, slightly darker) and sweeping **rose-crimson inlaid curved bands** (`#c97998` day; `#533b5d` at night) forming a pinwheel.
  - A **big 4-point star in rose `#c97998`** marks the **exact centre**, with curved tails. The boss flies to it for chaos.
  - The rim has **gold ornamental lamp spires** (star-topped, `#fffac2` glow).
  - Beyond: grass, trees, cliffs, waterfalls and a stone aqueduct arch.
  - **Radius**: rough estimate **≈18-25 m (≈6-8 BH)**. The boss covers ≈10-15 m in the 1.5 s flight to the centre, and the plaza edge is ≈10-12 BH away in long shots.
- **Camera**: Genshin's third-person follow cam, ≈4-5 m behind and slightly above the player, pitched ≈10-15° down. During chaos it pitches up (≈20-30°) to keep the hovering boss in frame. No cinematic cameras.

## 5. Visual details per mechanic

### 5.1 Guard-mode entry, "spins the spear around herself"
See `n_guardentry1_090-170.jpg`, `t_guardentry_quad.jpg`, `key_f114/138/160.jpg`.
- She **does not throw the spear and does not summon extra spears**. She twirls her one spear, and its trail draws **vertical golden discs**:
  - **Diameter ≈1.0-1.2 BH (≈3-3.5 m)**, centred ≈1-1.5 m from her.
  - The rim is bright gold `#fce484` / `#e4b43c` (≈0.1 m thick) with a white-hot edge.
  - The fill is translucent amber `#b49c54` / `#9c843c`, with a dark brown swirl inside at 2.3 s.
  - Discs appear in sequence: her right/screen-left (1.77-2.0), in front (2.03), around her body and right (2.1-2.37).
  - Gold ground sparks, **gold crystal spikes** and small dark-red cube debris kick up where each disc touches the floor.
- It ends with a **flat gold ring on the floor, radius ≈1.5 BH (≈4.5 m)** around her. Thin (≈0.15 m) line of `#fce484` sparks, expanding slightly and fading over ≈0.4 s.
- No player damage was observed for the entry, so treat it as a telegraph or ambient effect.

### 5.2 Guard-mode hold
See `b_180-500.jpg`, `key_f300.jpg`, `key_f1290.jpg`.
- **Pose**: spear held diagonally across her body, pointing **up and to her right** (screen up-left) at ≈35-45° above horizontal. Wings spread wide upward. She is nearly static and drifts slowly toward the player.
- **Aura**: the whole boss and spear glow gold (`#e4b43c` → `#fcfc84` highlights). Small floating **gold square motes** (≈0.15 m cubes, `#e4cc54`) drift around her.
- **With a recorded element** (guard II), the **pauldron/wing gem stars glow magenta-violet** (`#e484fc`, `#fae6fa` core) while the spear stays gold until the record completes.
- Recording progress shows only in the HUD icon (§3). There is no world-space counter.

### 5.3 Un-recorded (Geo/gold) end swing
See `o_goldswing_546-590.jpg`, `c_500-596.jpg`.
- **Wind-up**: ≈0.93 s / 19 t after the guard timer ends.
- **Arc**: ≈180-210° horizontal sweep at waist-to-ground height, from her left to her right. **Radius ≈1.5-1.7 BH (≈4.5-5 m)**: at the player's depth the FX span the full screen width (≈8 m).
- **Trail**: a thick, layered gold crescent (`#cc9c3c`, `#e4b43c`, `#e4b454`, highlights `#fcfc84`, white core `#fcfcfc`), ≈0.3-0.5 m thick.
- **Ground**: a row of **vertical gold crystal spikes** along the arc edge (0.5-1.5 m tall, `#fce484`) plus brown/gold rock chips and small dark-red cubes.
- **Timing**: active 5 t; FX gone after ≈0.45 s.

### 5.4 Electro-enchanted swing
See `f_880-1000.jpg`, `i_1420-1560.jpg`, `key_f1000.jpg`.
- **Record burst**: a violet-white starburst on her (≈0.5 BH).
- **Enchant**: a horizontal violet light line along the spear, or (guard II) a ring burst around her waist.
- **Wind-up**: spear raised high.
- **Sweep**: same geometry as the gold swing (≈180-210°, radius ≈4.5-5 m), but in **violet**: `#e484fc`, `#cc6cfc`, `#b454fc`, `#cc84fc`, deep `#6c54cc` / `#5454b4` in the shadows, white-pink core `#fce4fc`. Forked **lightning bolts** land on the ground inside the arc, and violet shards fly out.
- **Active window**: ≈0.4 s (8 t). Ground arcs persist ≈0.6-0.8 s.
- **Damage**: 7417 to Clorinde both times.
- **Timing from record complete to active**: 0.97 s (19-20 t) both times. From guard timeout (gold case) to active: 0.93 s.

### 5.5 Relocation to the centre (start of #7)
See `j_1560-1680.jpg`, `k_1680-1800.jpg`.
- **Order**: vertical leap → arcing flight with a **trail of red/black voxel cubes** (`#fc5454`, `#b4243c`, black edges, some gold-lit `#e4cc6c`) → heavy landing on the central star → **≈1.5 s charge** (red-orange spear-head flare, then a red orb and cube "+" sigil) → **vertical rise to hover**.

### 5.6 Chaos: element stars
See `key_f1801.jpg`, `key_f1836.jpg`, `u_starspawn_1770-1812.jpg`, `l_1780-1920.jpg`.
- **Count**: 2 stars, both Electro, since Electro was the only recorded element. This is consistent with "2 per active element".
- **Look**: each star is **two stacked violet cubes** (`#9d3ed6`, lit faces `#d282fa` / `#e6befa`, ≈0.45 m each, slightly rotated) topped by a **glowing pale-pink orb** (`#f3cef9`, ≈0.4 m). Total ≈1.1-1.3 m tall.
  - Each carries its own small red HP bar and an element-aura icon above it.
  - Later the cubes look like violet flame/crystal clusters, especially when hit.
- **Placement**: side by side ≈1.2-1.5 m apart, at the arena centre ≈2-3 m in front of (below) the hovering boss.
- **Feedback**: hits show "면역" (IMMUNE) and "감전" (electro-charged) text pop-ups.
- **Destroying a star**: it shatters, and a violet-white starburst appears on the boss. It deals 6 % max HP to her (§4 B27-B29).

### 5.7 Chaos: ambience and attacks
See `r_chaos_1920-2400.jpg`, `m_stars_1800-2400.jpg`, `key_f1878/2130/2370.jpg`.
- **Sky/backdrop tint**: the backdrop is washed **crimson** (a red dome: `#963246`, `#9c3c3c`, `#9c3c54`, `#b43c3c`). Red light shafts hang under the boss.
- **Red cube streams**: snake-like chains of 5-10 **red wireframe cubes** (`#b4243c` / `#fc5454` with black edges, ≈0.3-0.6 m), each led by a **gold-orange glowing cube** (`#e4cc54`) and leaving dark ember particles.
  - They arc out from around the hovering boss and sweep down through the arena around the player.
  - 2-5 streams are visible at any time, crossing the screen in ≈0.5-1 s (≈6-10 m/s).
  - The player keeps losing HP during this phase.
- **Red cross sigil** on the boss from ≈36 s (§4 B26).
