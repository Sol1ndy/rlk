  Korean caption at bottom-left (yellow bar + title + one-line description, see `caption_titles.png`). Clips come from

  night blue arena 14.7–31.5 s).

## 1. Boss identity, model, scale

* Nameplate (top-centre, `boss_nameplate.png`): **개안자** / subtitle **세계 망루의 파수꾼**, **Lv.103**, red HP bar
  (#FA575A-ish fill on a dark frame), a Hydro aura icon floats above the bar (applied by the player's party, not a boss
  mechanic). Player: Lv.90, 19268 max HP, party 린네아 / 클로린드 / 느비예트 / 푸리나.
* Model in game (matches ref.png): gold halo/crown ring behind the head (bright, with a 4-point sparkle on top),
  white hair/veil, red-black bodysuit with gold armour accents, long white feathered skirt panels, black-red legs.
  **One asymmetric wing assembly on her LEFT side (screen-right when she faces the camera)**: a large gold shield-plate
  with a central eye/gem (gold frame, dark-red inner, orange gem) from which a fan of long white feathers sweeps out
  and down. Spear in her RIGHT hand: long gold shaft, crimson tip + small red diamond ornaments, big gold crescent blade
  ("wing" shaped, 2 curved horns) near the head; red/black spear butt. (The task's ref note says "feathers on her
  left, shield on her right" — in-game it is one combined shield+feather wing; if the ref is mirrored, follow the video.)
* Locomotion: **never walks**. Idle = hovering, feet ~0.3–0.5 m above the floor, legs dangling, slight bob, spear held
  diagonally low across the body pointing down-forward-left, wing fanned. Moves by gliding / flying / dashing.
* Scale (from frames where boss and player are at ~equal depth, `measure_scale_after_pull_f1060.jpg`, f1060: player
  262 px tall, boss head-top→feet ~420 px, halo-top→feet ~478 px, boss ~1 m behind the player):
  * **Boss body height (head-top to feet) ≈ 1.6× player ≈ 2.6–2.8 m; with halo ≈ 1.8–2.0× ≈ 3.0–3.3 m.** Use
    **H_b ≈ 2.7 m (≈ 2.7 MC blocks)** as "1 boss height" below.
  * Spear length ≈ 1.2–1.4 H_b ≈ 3.5–4 m (crescent blade ≈ 0.25 H_b across).
  * Wing assembly span (from shoulder to feather tips) ≈ 1.2–1.4 H_b; shield-plate ≈ 0.4 H_b.
* Camera: Genshin third-person, behind the player ~6–9 m, pitch ~10–20° down; player lower-centre (230–360 px tall),
  boss upper-middle. One close-up at 5.30–5.75 s (player at the boss's feet).

  crimson / red terracotta) inlay rings and a 4-point star in the centre, gold (gold block / lantern / end-rod) posts on
  the rim.
