All images are in `understand/m4/`. Start with **`m4_keyframes.jpg`** (33 labelled stills). The full-res stills are `key_f####_<what>.jpg`.

## 1. Scale and camera

**Robust scale.** The player (Neuvillette, ≈1.65 m) stands 175 px tall (feet y≈840) for f0-160, which gives **≈106 px/m at the player's depth**. Every size below that is measured at the player's depth (spear height above the target, decal width, impact dome) uses this scale and does not depend on the camera model.

**Boss height.** This file keeps m2's **1 BH ≈ 3.0 m** (m1 measured 2.7 m).
- In m4 the hovering boss is ≈160 px from skirt tip to halo top at f30 (`x_f0030_boss_stars_player.png`).
- The two stars are ≈115 px tall at that point, ≈0.7 BH (≈2 m). They are larger than at spawn in m3, so they appear to grow during chaos.

**Camera.** Genshin follow cam, zoomed out, **almost level**.
- The arch pillars at screen right stay vertical (≤10 px lean over 450 px), so the pitch is ≲3-6°. A 25° pitch would lean them by ≈100 px.
- Assuming a 45° vertical FOV: the camera is ≈12 m behind the player, ≈3.4-4.2 m above the floor.
- **The player stands ≈12-19 m from the arena centre** (model range). m3's "5-6 m" for this attempt is an underestimate.
- The camera does not cut or play cinematics. It follows the player's two dodges (yawing right at f160-190 and left at f250-270).
- Over f300-420 it moves closer to the player: the player grows from 175 px to ≈310-330 px, either from a zoom scroll or from camera collision.
- The impacts produce **bloom/white-out**, not a measurable shake.

## 5. Mechanic details

### 5.1 Timer expiry → star absorption (T0 … +25 t)
See `g_stars_absorb_crop_40-102.jpg` (every 2 f) and `a_fail_start_crop_0-118.jpg` (every 3 f).

The sequence is fixed: **ring full → glow (6 t) → icon gone → stars flash white (+6.7 t) → stars become gold voxel clusters (+7…+10 t) → voxels darken to red-edged black cubes (+10…+14 t) → sigil breaks (+14.7 t) → two V-shaped cube chains fly up into her (+15…+17 t) → red gyroscope ring (+18 t) → white-gold 4-point starburst (+19…+21 t) → crimson smoke burst, red horizontal light line and haze collapse (+21…+25 t).**

- The stars **do not explode or damage anything**. They are "eaten" by the boss.
- The chaos **cube streams** stop at ≈+10…+15 t.
- The **sky haze** is removed at ≈+21…+25 t.

### 5.2 Giant spear (one per surviving star)
See `h_spear1_form_crop_100-176.jpg`, `b_spear1_full_114-200.jpg`, `i_spear2_boss_crop_196-268.jpg`, `e_spear2_full_189-300.jpg`.

- **Formation**: built from **gold cubes stacking vertically** (spear 1: under and through the boss; spear 2: high above the boss). A **red blade** then forms from the cubes. Formation takes ≈0.25-0.3 s (5-6 t).
- **Shape**: a **giant copy of her own spear, held vertically, blade/crescent down**. The shaft runs off the top of the screen.
  - **Planted head** (from `key_f0204/f0216/f0252`): tip to crescent tops ≈3.0-3.4 m; **crescent span ≈2 m**; red/orange star-diamond "eye" at the crescent root; shaft ≈0.3 m thick.
  - Visible length ≥8-10 m, i.e. **≈3× her normal 4.3-4.5 m spear**.
- **Hang**: vertical, over the **target position**, with the **lower tip ≈3.6-3.9 m (≈1.2-1.3 BH) above the floor**. It does not move except to stay over the target.
- **Colour progression while hanging**: always **starts red** (`#b40309` core), then shifts to the **colour of the star it came from**.
  - Spear 1 (gold star): red → gold in 8 f (f142-150), ≈0.58-0.45 s before impact.
  - Spear 2 (blue star): red → pink → violet → blue over f226-244, ≈0.72-0.42 s before impact.
  - With recorded elements the colours would presumably be the element's (Electro violet, Cryo cyan, etc.). This is not shown.
- **Drop**: **≤1 frame (<1 tick)**. There is no visible fall, just a flash column. The impact happens exactly at the decal centre.
- **Targeting**: the target is **the player's position, locked before the impact**.
  - Spear 1 lands where the player stood before the f160 dash, not where the player ended up.
  - Spear 2 lands where the player was before the f250 dash, ≈2.5 m right of the player's position at f268.
  - So the lock happens ≥0.3 s before impact, and probably **when the decal appears**.
- **Telegraph lengths**:

| spear | first cubes → impact | blade visible → impact | decal rim → impact | colour change complete → impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (gold) | f100 → f177 = **77 f = 1.28 s = 25.7 t** | f106 → f177 = 71 f = 23.7 t | f110 → f177 = **67 f = 1.12 s = 22.3 t** | f150 → f177 = 9 t |
| 2 (blue) | f196 → f269 = **73 f = 1.22 s = 24.3 t** | f205 → f269 = 64 f = 21.3 t | f220 → f269 = **49 f = 0.82 s = 16.3 t** | f244 → f269 = 8.3 t |

- **Order**: gold (left star) first, then blue (right star), **1.53 s (30.7 t) apart**. One spear per undestroyed star. The PDF says "slams powerful spears per undestroyed star".

### 5.3 Ground decal (telegraph)
See `d_diamond_floor_96-176.jpg`, `j_floor_spear2_196-268.jpg`, `key_f0108/f0120/f0140`.

- **Shape**: a **red concave 4-point star (rhombus-like) centred on the player**. One arm points at the arena centre (where the boss is).
  - The **far tip** sits exactly on the arena's floor-star centre, below the boss: fixed at y=616 from f112 to f176.
  - The **side arms** lie at the player's depth (dz ≈ 0 ± 0.6 m), so the shape is centred on the player, not on the arena centre.
- **Growth** (half-width of the side arms at the player's depth; robust to the camera model):

| f | s | half-width |
|---|---|---|
| 108 | 1.80 | small diamond only (≈±1.5-2 m) |
| 112 | 1.87 | **±2.6-2.8 m** |
| 116 | 1.93 | **±3.3-4.3 m** |
| 120 | 2.00 | **±6-7.7 m** |
| ≥126 | ≥2.10 | beyond the screen edge (**>±9 m**) |

  - The concave near-side arcs keep sweeping outward until impact.
  - At the screen bottom (≈4.5 m behind the player) the decal is still ≈±3.5 m wide, so the rear arm is ≥6-8 m.
  - Read it as a **≈20 m-wide red star opening in ≈0.3 s (6 t)**.
- **Look**: translucent crimson fill `#cc2848` (≈40-50 % alpha over the floor, deeper `#672a3e` on dark floor), a bright **pink-red rim** `#e25677`, and **short vertical pink light streaks** (≈0.5-1.5 m tall, `#872d4c` → `#e05475`) standing along the rims like a curtain of spikes.
- **Removed at the impact** (rim pixels gone by f186 / f278).
- **Damage zone vs decal.** The decal is huge, but the caption says the hit is **"not wide, easy to leave, dodgeable"**, and the player escaped with one ≈2-2.5 m sideways dash while still inside the red star. So the actual hit is the **impact dome**:
  - core radius ≈1.5 m; blast ≈2.5-3 m.
  - **Recommendation for MC**: draw the star decal as the telegraph, and apply damage in a **cylinder of r ≈ 2.5-3 m (≈0.9 BH) around the impact point**, with dash i-frames allowed.

### 5.4 Gold impact (spear 1)
See `c_spear1_impact_170-189.jpg` (every frame).
- **f177 (+0 f)**: column flash plus long starburst rays from the impact point; the screen is lifted ≈+45 % in luminance.
- **f180-183 (+3…+6 f)**: an **egg/dome of solid gold light**, ≈2.9 m wide and tall, sitting on the impact point.
- **f183-192 (+6…+15 f)**: the dome blows out into a **white-gold blast** (luminance ≈2× normal).
  - Two **crossed gold light beams (an X)** ≈8-10 m tall rise from the impact.
  - A **ring/field of vertical gold crystal spikes** (1-3 m tall, thin) erupts out to ≈±8 m.
- **f192-216 (+15…+39 f)**: fade over ≈0.4 s.
  - Brown rock chunks are thrown 2-5 m high (Geo flavour).
  - Gold 4-point sparkles float.
  - Golden haze lingers to ≈f230.

### 5.5 Blue impact (spear 2)
- **f269**: blue-white flash column and rays.
- **f275-283**: white-blue blast plus a **blue X of crossed beams**.
- **f284-354**: **white-blue splash spikes** (water/ice look, 1-2.5 m) erupt over ≈±4 m. Blue glowing floor cracks and patches persist to ≈f354 (5.9 s).
- Same timing structure as the gold impact: **flash at +0, blast at +6…+14 f, residue ≈1.2 s**.

### 5.6 Planted spear and floor residue
- After each impact the **giant spear stays planted head-down**, with the crescent and blade at floor level and the shaft rising out of view.
  - Gold spear: f177 → dissolves top-down into **gold/dark cubes floating upward** f238-270. **Lifetime ≈1.5 s (30 t)**; dissolve ≈0.5 s.
  - Blue spear: f269 → dissolves into **violet/pink cubes** ≈f330-366. Lifetime ≈1.5 s.
- **Floor**: crimson tint `#572520` / `#782524` spreads from the gold impact (f206-230, up to ≈35 % of the lower screen). It is criss-crossed by **glowing gold crack lines** `#f8d425` radiating ≥8 m. It is replaced by the blue residue after impact 2 and gone by f274-300.

### 5.7 Boss movement and poses during the fail sequence
- **T0 … +25 t**: hovering over the centre (feet ≈1-1.3 BH up). She turns, absorbs the stars, curls into the starburst, then **rises ≈0.5-0.7 BH**.
- **+25 … +49 t**: hangs beside the giant spear, then **descends slowly** (wings raised, body upright).
- **≈+55 t**: **standing on the floor at the centre**.
- **+60 … +79 t**: summoning spear 2. First her spear is held horizontally behind her shoulders; then she **raises it vertically overhead** in the last ≈0.3 s before the drop.
- **+90 … +126 t**: **glides to the player** (hover ≈0.3 m), spear levelled at the player, then a diagonal raise and lower.
  - Closing speed ≈2-4 m/s; the exact distance cannot be measured because the camera zooms in at the same time.
- **+126 t →**: at melee range (≈2 m) in the forward-low spear stance. This is the normal combat idle seen in m1/m2/m3.
- She **never attacks with her own spear** during the fail state. All damage comes from the falling spears.

### 5.8 Player behaviour (for difficulty tuning)
- The player stands still until ≈0.3 s before impact 1, then **dashes sideways ≈2-2.5 m** (f160-176) and is not hit.
- The player repeats this for spear 2 (f250-268).
- No damage numbers appear on the player.
- Both "leave the range" and "dodge with i-frames" work, matching the caption.
