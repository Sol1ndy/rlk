## For S2 (rig + sequences)
- The core calls `rig/tick` **after** the module logic every tick (intro, fight, death), so the shown clip tick == the pattern
  clock `#pt` (play at #pt 0 shows tick 0 the same game tick).
- The core owns: music 1 at intro tick 176, the intro push ring at 178, the T1 push ring at `flare` 50, music 2 at T2 `swap` 60,
  `rlk_mb_inv` during T1 / T2, `core/looks` after T1 / T2 (form / visor / edge / chest off / hands grip / state normal: idempotent
  look calls). **seq must not repeat these.** seq owns the title at the intro ignite (156), the T2 blindness + arena look job at
  `dark` 50 (Service docstring), the death sound (registry `deathSnd:false`) and every look change inside the sequences.
- The body (anchor) stands on the floor at the arena centre for the whole intro (spawn); the hang / drop is the rig's offset.
- `seq/<name>/cancel` is called with **no executor** from cleanup and from death / reload (make it executor-agnostic). A reload
  during the intro calls `seq/intro/cancel` then jumps to the fight; during T1 / T2 the core finishes the transition itself.
- Looks the core uses: `look/form/{cape,winged,abyss}`, `look/visor/{up_off,down_pyro,down_electro,down_abyss}`,
  `look/edge/{off,pyro,electro,glint}`, `look/chest/{off,red}`, `look/hands/{open,grip}`, `state/normal`; clips: every melee clip,
  `walk strafe_l strafe_r stagger stun swap_pyro swap_electro mantle_in mantle_hold mantle_break mantle_nova intro t1_wings
  t2_abyss death`.
