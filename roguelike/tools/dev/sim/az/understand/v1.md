## 2. Size: Azhdaha against the player (the main deliverable of v1)

### 2.1 Method
* Pinhole camera, principal point at the image centre, Genshin **vertical FOV 45° (f = 1304 px)**.
* **Frame f2310 (38.50 s)** has two marker rings on flat floor plus Barbara standing in one of them. Each ring's
  minor/major axis ratio gives its depression angle: M2 437×203 px → 27.7°, M1 276×72 px → 15.1°. Their difference
  (12.6°) matches the row difference under f = 1190–1304 px, which confirms the FOV at 45–49°. Camera pitch comes out at
  **15.4° down**.
* Barbara's height in px (593→812) then gives **camera height 4.1 m** and **Barbara 8.0 m away**.
* Cross-checks: both rings come out the same physical size (Ø 3.0 and 3.2 m), and Barbara stands at the ring-2 centre
  (8.0 vs 8.0 m). Outputs: `geom.py`, `measure_f2310_annot.jpg`.
* **Frame f2202 (36.70 s):** the camera sits almost on the floor, looking up (horizon row ≈ 793–796, camera ≈ 0.2 m
  high, pitch 11° up). Barbara's feet (row 829) and the boss's front claws (row 810) sit on almost the same image row,
  but this is **not** the same depth: with a floor-level camera, 19 px of row means about 10 m of depth. The f2202 solve
  is therefore constrained by the Barbara→front-feet gap taken from f2310 (10.1–10.9 m; both stood still from 36.0 to
  38.5 s). It then yields heights for the raised pose (`geom2202.py`, `measure_f2202_annot.jpg`).
* **Frame f2060 (34.33 s):** idle front view. The front claw span (570 px = 11 m) is the ruler for the other features at
  the same depth (`measure_f2060_grid.jpg`).
* Error budget: Barbara's height ±5 %, FOV ±5 %, ellipse and edge reading ±5 %, features behind the claw plane ±10 %.
  Overall **±12–15 %**.

## 5. Model notes

### 5.1 Parts and colours
Sampled hex values come from frames in hazy arena light, so in-game colours read greyer and darker than the official
art. Use the wiki portrait palette for saturated albedo, and these values as the "in arena" check.

* **Overall build:** a massive, low quadruped. In combat it carries its head **low between the forelegs** (chin almost on
  the floor). Very thick columnar forelegs set wide (11 m claw span); the hind legs are smaller and further back. The
  back is a crown of tall dark plates. In the intro it rears semi-upright on its hind legs (`k_f1740`).
* **Head** (`zoom_f2352_head_side.jpg`, `zoom_f2310_head_grid.jpg`, `k_f2370`):
  * Broad, blunt, crocodile/lion-like, ≈ 5.5 m across the cheek plates.
  * A dark umber **crest of plates in a V over the brow** with a central nose-ridge plate pointing down:
    #4F3022 / #3B251A / #4F3020.
  * **Glowing red eyes** under the brow: core #C83037 / #BE353C, hot centre #FB7F80, in-intro glow #E54F50. The
    portrait's amber eyes do not apply in game: in combat they are red.

    back**. Lit #B48E39 / #C29B34, mid #906A37 / #A36C29, shadow #6F4622 / #77421B, brightest #C8A43E, inner horn face
