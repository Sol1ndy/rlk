## 1. Size of Azhdaha (Task 3)

### 1.1 Method and why the error bars are what they are
* Pinhole camera, principal point at the image centre, Genshin vertical FOV 45° (f = 1304 px), no roll. Ruler: **Barbara ≈ 1.55 m**
  (her measured screen height; results are given in metres and in **PH = player heights of 1.7 m**, the MC ruler).
* `v9_tools/levelcam.py`: for a camera pitch θ, the player's head/feet rows give the camera height and the player's distance, the
  boss's ground-contact row gives the boss distance, and each boss point is placed on the vertical through that ground point (heights)
  or at that distance (widths). Running poses are scaled ×0.9 (a running Barbara is ≈10 % shorter on screen).
* **The pitch is the dominant unknown.** Nothing in v9 gives a clean horizon: the floor seals are too complex to identify parallel
  edges by hand, the take-C water ring's far edge is hidden behind the boss, and the hail streaks give inconsistent tilts
  (−2…+5°). For a frame where the boss stands 2× farther than the player, sizes change by ×2 between θ = 0° and θ = 12°.
* **The pitch-robust frame is `full/f0240.png` (4.00 s, `measure_f0240_annot.jpg`)**: Barbara runs right in front of the boss's right
  front leg, her feet (row 830) only 50 px below the paw contact (row 780), so the boss is only 1–2.5 m behind her and the result
  barely depends on θ (0°…15° → eyes 2.19…1.94 m, crown 4.8…4.1 m). That frame fixes the scale; the other frames are then read at
  the pitch that agrees with it, **θ ≈ 12° (8–15°)**, which is also what the v1 agent measured with two floor rings (15.4°, camera
  4.1 m, Barbara 8 m away) — the normal Genshin chase camera. The eye height of ≈2.0 m in the head-low stance agrees with v1 (2.0 m)
  and v2 (2.0–2.15 m).
