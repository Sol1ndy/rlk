## 1. Size: Azhdaha against the player

### 1.1 Method
* Pinhole camera with the principal point at the image centre and the Genshin **vertical FOV 45° (f = 1304 px)**. v1.md
  confirmed 45–49° from two floor rings.
* **Calibrated frame f1950 (32.50 s), the stomp's 3rd ring at full size.** I traced 24 points on the gold ring's near edge
  (`grid_f1950_ring*.jpg`) and fitted camera pitch, camera height, ring centre and ring radius together, with Barbara
  (feet row 822, hat top 593) as the only length ruler (`fit_f1950.py`, rms 3.8 px):
  * **pitch 12.8° down, camera 3.7 m high, Barbara 7.9 m from the camera**
  * **ring radius 10.4 m** (11.3 m if the ruler is 1.7 m), ring centre 12.2 m from Barbara, i.e. 1.8 m beyond her
  * Heights at the stomping foot's depth: `measure1950.py`.
* **Other frames** (`framemeasure.py`, configs `meas_f*.json`): camera height comes from the player's pixel height, depth from
  a ground-contact row, both scanned over pitch 10–14°. The table gives the 12° value and the 10–14° range; 12° matches the
  fitted 12.8° and v1's 15°.
* **Side view f0705** (11.75 s): points are intersected with the body's vertical plane through both near feet, 1.5 m behind
  them (`sideplane.py`).
* **Error budget:**
  * ruler height ±5 %
  * FOV ±5 %
  * pitch ±2°: the largest term, ±10–25 % for far features
  * a feature off the contact plane: ±10 %
  * **overall ±15 % near the boss's feet, ±25 % for the far side and the tail**
  * f1950 is better: ±8 %
