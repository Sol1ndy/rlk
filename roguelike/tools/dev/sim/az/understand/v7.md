## 1. Boss identity and scale (Task 3)

### 1.1 Method
Pinhole camera over a flat floor. For a player of known height (Barbara ≈ 1.60 m, Kaeya ≈ 1.85 m; tall-male model) and the
boss's ground-contact row, scale at the boss = (player px / player m) × (y_boss_feet − y_horizon) / (y_player_feet −
y_horizon). The horizon is not visible (the arena floor ends at a fogged edge), so each frame gets a horizon range bounded
by the far floor edge (the true horizon is above it) and by a level camera (row 540). This range is the main error term.
The calculation is in `v7_tools/size_est.py` → `size_est.txt`; grids are in `measure_f0000_grid.jpg`, `measure_f1150_grid.jpg`,
`measure_f2075_grid.jpg` and `measure_f0606_grid.jpg`.
