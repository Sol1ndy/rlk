# A_catalog — Raiden boss (마가츠 미타케 나루카미노 미코토), footage v1-v5 (analyst A)

Scope: v1-v5 only. **All five videos show only the humanoid Raiden Shogun form.** Neither 악요의 요고우 텐마 (the large
armoured form with extra arms) nor the 만겁 / 금강괴 / 흉장의 수안 mechanics appear in v1-v5. The source is a Korean
pattern-guide compilation with a title caption per pattern (quoted, used as the creator's pattern names) and a 2x slow replay
after most patterns. All timings below come from the real-time parts only.

Units: `t` = Minecraft ticks (20 t/s). "tell" = first readable cue (motion, flash or sound) to the first damaging frame.
Sizes are in blocks (b), scaled from the player (about 1.8 b) and the boss (about 1.9 b, the same height as Xingqiu); grid
measurements are in `work/A/fr/grid1.jpg`, and other sizes are +-30 % estimates. Audio onsets come from a 1.5-8 kHz
spectral-flux detector (`work/A/onset.py`); they line up with the visuals within about 0.1 s, except v5 4-6 s, where the
thunder tail lags. Per-video logs: `A_v1.md` … `A_v5.md`.

Recurring tell vocabulary (seen across many attacks):
- **Star flash**: a bright white/white-violet 4-point star glint at the weapon tip or her hip, 0.4-0.8 s before the hit (뇌명, 원거리 검기, 번개돌진, 4단).
- **Polearm twirl**: she spins the polearm overhead, drawn as a vertical bright ring about 1.5 b across (polearm rear check, 번개 폭발, 돌진 3콤보).
- **Hoop + vortex**: a dark vertical hoop around her, then a violet swirl disc at her feet (both weapon switches).
- **Blink**: she dissolves into violet flicker, is invisible about 6 t, then reappears in a violet smoke cloud with speed streaks.

## Model (humanoid Raiden Shogun; the only form in v1-v5)

Reference crops: `frames/A/v1_12.50_model_polearm_vs_sword.png`, `v1_16.50_model_sword_closeup.png`,
`v4_09.00_model_polearm_closeup.png`, `v4_28.85_blink_reappear.png`, `v1_00.50_intro_vortex.png`.

- **Silhouette and proportions**: a slender adult woman about 1.9 b tall (player height + 5-10 %). Narrow shoulders; a long back drape and wide sleeves make her outline widen below the waist. Head about 1/7.5 of her height. Legs long and dark (stockings), so the lower half reads dark and the torso pale.
- **Hair**: very dark indigo-violet (about #2e2a4f, highlights #4b4580), a long single braid down the back to about hip level, a full fringe, and a pale violet flower ornament on the left side of the head. The face is mostly shadowed with the head slightly bowed; the eyes are not readable at gameplay distance.
- **Clothing**:
  - Upper body: a pale lavender/white kimono (about #d8cfe6 with violet shading #9b8cc0), with wide drooping sleeves to below the hips. The right sleeve is longer and darker violet.
  - Waist: a deep plum/maroon obi-corset (about #5a2a40) with a small gold/purple knot ornament and a short tassel at the front.
  - Skirt: short pale front panels over the thighs.
  - Back drape: a long dark violet translucent drape or train (about #4a3a70, 60-70 % opacity) from the waist to the ankles at the back and sides.
  - Legs and feet: black-violet thigh-high stockings (#1d1b2b) and black heeled boots.
- **Halo (floating, both weapons)**: a vertical arc behind her upper back, about 1.8 b across.
  - Tomoe orbs: 4 violet "tomoe" orbs, each about 0.35 b, glowing #8f5cff with a darker core. Two sit at head height and two at chest height, about 0.9 b to each side of the spine.
  - Eye emblem: a diamond/winged-lens "eye" (violet with a white rim, about 0.6 b wide) floats at the top, about 0.9 b above the head.
  - Thin lightning threads link them. The arc hovers and rotates slightly but does not orbit.
- **Sword (Musou Isshin)**: a slim long katana about 1.4-1.5 b, held low in the right hand with the tip pointing down and forward near the floor. The blade glows pale violet-white with a lightning crackle; the dark hilt has a violet ornament. For iai attacks she brings it to her left hip.
- **Polearm (naginata/glaive)**: about 2.2-2.5 b long, held slanted in the right hand. A dark violet shaft with ornate rings; a curved glowing violet blade at the lower end in idle (pointing down near the floor) and a short spike above her head. She twirls it overhead (drawn as a vertical bright ring about 1.5 b) for several tells.
- **Weapon switch visual**: a dark vertical hoop surrounds her, then becomes a flat violet disc and swirl at her feet; the weapon changes during the burst.
- **Motion style**:
  - Idle: very still, upright, weight on one leg, weapon low; she can stand idle 1-2 s between attacks (v1 37.7-39.4).
  - Movement: no walking in the fight footage; distance is covered with blinks (dissolve and reappear in violet smoke) or lightning dashes.
  - Attacks: crisp and sudden. A short frozen stance (iai at the hip, or the polearm raised) is punctuated by a star-glint flash, then a burst of very large FX much bigger than her body.
  - Stagger: hunched or kneeling on a dark violet disc.
- **Shared FX palette**:
  - Electro violet #9b5cff, deep violet #3a1f6b (crystals, smoke), bright lilac rims #c9a6ff, white-pink flashes #f6e0ff, blue-white charge glow #d8e8ff.
  - Crystal shards are dark violet, jagged and angular, with bright edges.
  - The floor in the main arena is teal (#5fb0b0) with ripple patterns, which makes violet effects contrast strongly.
- **Arena**: the main arena is an apparently endless flat teal floor (Plane of Euthymia), with the edge rarely in frame; its size is taken to be large (at least 20 b radius). The intro area is a night shrine with two dark red pillars and a huge violet swirling vortex behind the boss (the entry, not combat). v4 11.7-20.4 is filmed in a different arena with ornate gold hanging frames.

## Open questions
1. **악요의 요고우 텐마 form**: not present in v1-v5. The large armoured form, its patterns and the 만겁 / 금강괴 mechanics have to come from v6-v9 (other analyst) or the wiki.
2. **A-02 pale disc**: it is read here as the sword-switch field-lightning marker. It overlapped the rear check (A-03) in time, so the two could be one attack. The v1 21.2 disc had no visible bolt before the cut. The strike cadence and count are unknown.
3. **A-03 / A-12 "rear"**: from the footage alone it is not clear whether the line or band always points behind her (wiki 판결의 검: triggers when attacked from behind). Exact aiming is assumed.
4. **A-09**: v4 0.0 is assumed to continue from v3 40.4 (no frame overlap found). The total cast length (about 138 t) depends on this. The orb count is about 5 (from audio) and the decal radius is at least 4 b (cut by the frame).
5. **A-18**: the Gaze explosion timer and damage radius if not broken are not shown; the 5 b ring decal is taken as the blast radius. Whether the boss stagger after the break matches A-08 (about 64 t) is not shown.
6. **A-16 wiki mapping** is uncertain (영원함의 꿈 vs 뇌명 돌진 (창)). A-17 seems to combine 뇌명 돌진 (창) (lightning-shaped dash) and 영원함의 꿈 (swing, then dash).
7. **Sizes** are perspective estimates (+-30 %), apart from the grid-measured A-17 ring (about 4.5 b), A-18 ring (about 5 b) and A-15 blast (at least 5.5 b).
8. **Damage, i-frame timing and HP** of the Gaze orb and clones cannot be read from the footage.
9. **5-hit sword combo (A-05)** has no exact wiki entry. The wiki's 원류 (4 hits) is the polearm chain (A-13).
