Meanings: 1 = "mob phase: kill mobs to fill the gauge"; 2 = "gauge management: if the gauge is fully used up you are forced to
fail"; 3 = "lowers the gauge with melee or ranged attacks"; 4 = "buffs basic organisms and gains overwhelming resistance; when
the buffed basic organisms are killed it self-destructs"; 5 = "puts a shield on the basic organisms (sic: 유기체) around it;
the shield is almost impossible to break, so the guarding organism must be destroyed first".

**Clarification for the 양육 rules** (caption 4 + wiki table 14.png): the **nurturing organism itself** gets "모든 내성 9910 %"
while it buffs (that row of the table is the 양육 one, "버프 중"); the buffed basics stay damageable (Wanderer's burst hits them
for 20471 each at 24.2–25.6 s). Killing the buffed basics costs the 양육 40 % max HP each (wiki) → after the third it dies
("자멸"). Seen in v3: 3 buffed basics die at **25.617 s** (bar +5.2 % = 3 × 1.75 %) and the 양육 self-destructs at **25.700 s**
(bar +11.6 % ≈ +11 %), 1.7 ticks later. The wiki.md transcription's advice "kill the buffed basics' source first" does not match the game: kill the
**buffed basics**, the source (immune) dies by itself.

## 8. Implementation-oriented summary (MC ticks, 20 tps, 1 m = 1 block)

* **Stage start**: bar at **25 %** (wiki ≈ 27 %), message "마물을 처치해서 소생 진행도를 올리세요" for ≈ 100–120 t; first group of
  ≈ 4–5 basics fades in (5 t) at 12–18 blocks from the Heart, at ≈ t+20.
* **Progress**: +1.75 % per basic kill, +11 % per other kill; −0.6 % per hit on the Heart, −1.2 % per buffed hit; **≤ 0 % →
  fail 2 t later** (screen darken 0 t → "도전 실패" +5 t → title up +30 t → panel +40 t). Bar = gold bossbar.
* **Basic**: walk to the Heart (~0.1–0.15 b/t, bobbing; ignore the player); either **melee** from contact (one hit per 40–80 t)
  or stop at 10–14 blocks, lie down (bag mode) and **shell**: pivot 3 t → crown glow 2 t → crown open 2 t → spark 2 t → **launch
  at +9 t** → arcing shell (apex 3–4, ≈ 0.85 b/t, 15 t flight) → hit. 1 shot per ≥ 20 t.
* **양육**: spawns stationary, immune (RES 9910 % ≈ invulnerable) while ≥ 1 tethered basic lives; tethers up to 3 basics
  (buff: hits −1.2 %, more poise); each buffed basic killed → 양육 −40 % max HP; after the 3rd it self-destructs (+2 t),
  progress +11 %. Visual: green cylinder ring + tether particles.
* **수호**: stationary 4.6-block spire; shield cast ≈ 44 t (collapse 9 → bud 12 → regrow 12 → +6 shields pop → +5 settle);
  shields on basics within ≥ 10 blocks; shields "almost unbreakable" → in MC: absorb ~all damage until the 수호 dies (or a very
  large shield HP). Recast interval unknown from v3.
* **Elemental-sight silhouettes** (`zoom_f1950_*`, `k_f1200_*`, `k_f2010_*`) are the cleanest outlines for the display models.
