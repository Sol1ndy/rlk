## R-S4-1 (S3, s3lib) — `s3lib.TID` has no `q4`
`TID = {k: tid for tid, k in api.TEST_IDS.items() if k in api.P1 + api.P2}` misses q4 because `api.TEST_IDS[45]` is
`'q4 (3 shots)'`; the core then cannot wire q4 (`S.TID[k]` KeyError at build). **Stand-in**: `p2.py` adds the entry at import
(`S.TID.setdefault('q4', 45)`, before the core is built). **Fix**: build TID from `n.split(' ')[0]` in s3lib (the p2 line then
does nothing); the same for `pat_p1.py`'s `T.S_TID` (knsim tests).

## R-S4-2 (S3, test 45) — "q4 (3 shots)"
`p/q4/start` fires 3 shots when `#tst kn` is 45 (the test id) or `#mbLock mb` is 45 (the lock), and **resets `#tst` to 0**
(consumed: otherwise every later AI-picked q4 of that session would fire 3). Cleaner: `test/go/q4` sets `#q4_t3 kn 1` before
`core/start/q4`; tell S4 / the lead and `p2.py` reads that instead.

## R-S4-6 (lead) — deviations from PLAN §6 (each from the user's standing lessons or a contract rule)
- **Q1**: from beyond 4.5 b a 10 t gliding lunge to 2.4 b in front of the player opens the cast (the AI picks Q1 at mid range,
  weight 15; "melee only from where it reaches"); h6 is stepped into first (the +-15 deg pair straddles a standing player
  farther than ~4.5 b). The crescents' return legs damage in the **same** volley group as the throw (one landing per throw).
  The cut after h3 / h5 is decided 2 t before the hit (its knockback: the last hit pushes); a cut cast waits (idle2) until its
  crescent is back.
- **Q2**: the wheel flies at most 10 t (15 b) so its burst strikes >= 10 t before the slash (COMBO_GAP); the wheel and its
  burst are one volley group. The dash goes to **1.5 b in front of the player's spot at go - 1** (<= `DASH_LEN` 14), not to the
  wheel's lock, and the slash is **skipped** (cut to idle2) when the player is beyond 6 b of the dash end (no whiff).
  Proposal: `DASH_LEN['q2']` 14 -> 20 so the far rule's targets (> 14 b) are always reached.
- **Q3**: the far follow-up is two crescents at **+-6 deg** (not +-15: at 9 b they would straddle a standing player), thrown
  12 t after the spin on its follow-through inside the q3 clip (no second clip); the near follow-up plays q1 h1-h3 (with the
  lunge from 4.5-6 b).
- **Q4**: no scorch decal (P2 decals are only ring_o / donut_o); the beam's knockback only on the last shot.
- **Q5B**: she steps 0.15 b / t toward the player during the rapid slashes while farther than 3 b; the final's tell is the
  crimson aura 20 t before it (76).
- **Q6**: one feather per volley (one HITS entry each), visible 14 t (lands at v + 14); the 8 orbs at fixed bearings k x 45 deg.
- **Q0**: placed (tp, she is hidden after t_p2's launch) at the hover spot 14 b from the player on the player -> her line (else
  turned / nearer, mv/ok), 10 b up; the trigger compares floor positions (a jump in place is no move). The blade ring(3,7)
  strikes 8 t after the impact disc(3) (PLAN: separate groups; the areas are complementary, overlap 2.7-3.3 b).
